01 / OVERVIEW

A simpler way to manage child health records.

⌘

About the project

Anganwadi centres play an important role in supporting children's health and development. Maintaining records and keeping track of vaccination schedules can become difficult when information is scattered across manual records.

This project provides a centralized digital interface for maintaining children's records, viewing vaccination information, and helping workers identify upcoming vaccinations and follow-up needs.

THE CHALLENGE

Making follow-up easier

  • Keeping children's information organized
  • Tracking vaccination status and schedules
  • Identifying upcoming vaccinations
  • Reducing dependence on scattered records
✳
PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Turn health information into actionable records.

Build a practical system that makes it easier for workers to maintain child records, monitor vaccination progress, and support timely follow-ups.
